# Ticket Execution Flow

When working on any ticket in this repository, you MUST strictly follow this workflow:

1. **Analyze in depth as much as you can**: Fully understand the codebase, the ticket, and the dependencies before writing code.
2. **Ground your work with proof**: Do not invent or assume things. Use native tools to verify state (read files, run commands, check types).
3. **Research when necessary**: Do web searches if you need more insight or ideas, especially regarding React Three Fiber or Next.js static export edge cases.
4. **Test until you can't test anymore**: Be exhaustive. Do not be myopic with your approach or test cases/edge cases. 
5. **Audit your work**: Always re-audit your work for errors and missingness, keeping things grounded with proof and no assumption or invention.

*Evidence before assertions, always.*
